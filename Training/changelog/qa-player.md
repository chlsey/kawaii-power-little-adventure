## Current position

Last reconciled: 2026-11-07. All recorded training and evaluation runs below are complete or explicitly halted.

- **Control design:** deterministic map/goal/route/recovery orchestrator plus one learned `local-waypoint-v1` movement policy. Waypoints are instructions; only the actual exit completes the integrated task. The combined positive waypoint budget is +5 per attempt, below the current +50 objective reward. Combat/competing-objective priority and learned orchestration are not implemented or validated. [Detailed contract](../PCG-system-design.md#overall-navigation-and-action-design).
- **Latest trained skill checkpoint:** QA-32, 492/500 training arrivals and 99/100 frozen arrivals at 8-12 units, successful frozen mean 2.99 s. Successful branch QA-27 -> QA-30 -> QA-31 -> QA-32, **63,246 branch training steps / 7,000 actor-lineage episodes**. QA-28/29's farther-distance fork is preserved separately and excluded from those counters.
- **Latest complete-task comparison:** QA-33/34 used the same frozen QA-32 weights and 500 exact matched distant-exit placements. Navigator enabled **364/500 (72.8%)**, disabled **236/500 (47.2%)**, advantage **+25.6 percentage points**. Mean episode durations including timeouts: **31.09 / 37.52 s**; on the 189 common successes, disabled was faster **11.75 / 19.52 s**. This is a guidance-removal ablation, not independent policy training. [Paired evidence](../../SavedModels/qa/local-waypoint-v1/diagnostics/no-navigator-20261107-230050-evaluate/README.md).
- **Scope and open work:** empty rooms only for these learned-policy results. Obstacle traversal, combat/local interruptions, generated-dungeon integration, waypoint ONNX deployment and timeout/truncation correction remain unvalidated or unimplemented. No model promotion or automatic curriculum advancement follows from the audit.

## What the stuck penalty actually changed

The controller's existing `StuckEvents` counter checks displacement every 20 actions and increments when net movement is less than 0.05 units. It is a diagnostic count, not a count of distinct collisions.

QA-08 added a separate reward rule: after 20 consecutive requested-movement actions within 0.05 horizontal units of an anchor, subtract **0.01 per action** until displacement reaches that threshold or movement is no longer requested. Episode reset clears the timer and totals; completion/death do not receive this penalty. Runtime checks verified the grace period, wall pushing, escape, and reset behavior. [Verification](../../Logs/qa-player-stuck-penalty-continuation/20261031-073035/reward-verification.json).

This narrower detector can reset despite high periodic stuck counts. Its low activation rate does not prove that wall-pushing behavior disappeared. A rolling requested-versus-actual movement detector has been discussed but not implemented.
